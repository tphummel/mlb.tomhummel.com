Method & caveats

  • Weekly: taken directly from OnRoto's own weekly standings in each Sunday snapshot (the last of each week). Missing snapshots on Mon Apr 27 and Mon May 4 don't affect any week. The opening week (Mar 25–29) is excluded as partial.
  • Monthly, counting stats (HR, NSB, RBI+R−HR, SO−BB, SV+HO−BS, W+QS): exact — month-end year-to-date minus previous month-end.
  • Monthly, rate stats (OBP, ERA): OnRoto publishes no PA or IP, so these are estimated by differencing year-to-date values and assuming each team's playing time accrues evenly by day. This reproduces OnRoto's own week-over-week figures closely for OBP and reasonably for ERA, but is not exact.
  • Sensitivity: using only the six exact counting stats, every monthly winner is unchanged except March–April (Hummel Dingers would win); July (The G-Spots would win). Months tagged “rate-stat dependent” or “close” are the ones sensitive to the OBP/ERA estimate.
  • Month boundaries use snapshot dates; a snapshot reflects that day's games. Within a whole month, a week may straddle two months; months are scored on calendar days, independent of the weekly results.
