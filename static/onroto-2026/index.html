Method & caveats

  • Weekly: taken directly from OnRoto's own weekly standings in the last snapshot of each week (normally Sunday's). Missing snapshots on Mon Apr 27 and Mon May 4 don't affect any week.
  • Best single days: a day's total is the change in a team's year-to-date total between two consecutive daily snapshots, so it is only as exact as OnRoto's nightly figures (later stat corrections can show up as part of a day). Skipped as not clearly one day: the first snapshot of the season, any day whose previous day has no snapshot and the day after a league-wide quiet day (9 days this season). A quiet day is one where all teams together moved under 40% as much as on a typical day. It is either a no-games day or a snapshot taken before OnRoto finished updating, and in the second case the next day would carry both days.
  • Results reflect each week's last snapshot, so any later OnRoto stat corrections are not included.
